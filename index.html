Legal Data Analytics

Medical Case Analytics

A text-processing workflow that turns unstructured Massachusetts court filings into traceable medical-debt insights.

TimelineFall 2025
SkillsPython, Regex, Fuzzy Matching, EDA, Data Visualization
10 yearscourt filings→structured cases

Project Overview & Description

A collaborative legal-data project exploring medical case categories in Massachusetts court filings. My contribution combined keyword identification, support for a regex and fuzzy-matching pipeline, and exploratory analysis of filing patterns across 2015–2025.

Objective

Turn unstructured court records into analyzable medical case categories, then identify changes over time and concentrations in creditor activity.

Approach

  • Identified relevant keywords to support classification of medical cases.
  • Contributed to a regex and fuzzy-matching workflow for unstructured court records.
  • Compared filing trends across medical categories and examined each creditor’s share of cases.

Results & Key Findings

  • Ambulance-related cases declined by 96% over 2015–2025, while other medical categories remained stable.
  • Trinity EMS accounted for 60% of ambulance cases.
  • MSPCA Angell represented 91% of veterinary filings.

Impact & Conclusion

The analysis made category-specific trends and creditor concentration visible within a large collection of court records. These descriptive findings provide a clearer starting point for further investigation; filing patterns alone do not establish their causes.

Healthcare Machine Learning

Stroke Risk Prediction

A comparative modeling study that combines supervised prediction and patient segmentation to identify meaningful stroke-risk profiles.

TimelineFall 2025
SkillsClassification, XGBoost, MLP, K-Means, PCA, GMM, Model Evaluation
14,000+patient records0.885 AUC

Project Overview & Description

A healthcare modeling study using 14,000+ patient records and 19 health variables. The project combines supervised stroke-risk prediction with unsupervised analysis to explore distinct patient risk profiles.

Objective

Compare classifiers using AUC, accuracy, and F1-score, while using dimensionality reduction and clustering to understand differences among patient groups.

Approach

  • Analyzed patient records across 19 health variables.
  • Evaluated Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, KNN, and MLP classifiers.
  • Applied K-Means, Gaussian mixture modeling, and PCA to investigate patient segments.

Results & Key Findings

  • Reported predictive performance of 0.885 AUC, 80.3% accuracy, and 0.836 F1-score.
  • Identified three distinct patient risk profiles through clustering and dimensionality reduction.

Impact & Conclusion

The study brings together predictive evaluation and patient segmentation, illustrating how both can contribute to understanding health data. These are project-level results; clinical use would require external validation and a separate assessment of deployment suitability.

Quantitative Finance

Portfolio Optimization

A forecasting and asset-allocation workflow that connects LSTM return estimates with Black–Litterman portfolio construction.

TimelineSpring 2024
SkillsR, LSTM, Black–Litterman, Portfolio Analytics
100 ETFs→Top 20by Sharpe ratio

Project Overview & Description

A quantitative finance project combining LSTM stock-price forecasting with Black–Litterman portfolio optimization in R. Risk and performance were benchmarked against 30 leading stocks and 100 ETFs.

Objective

Connect model-based forecasts to portfolio allocation and evaluate the resulting strategy using risk-adjusted metrics, including the Sharpe ratio.

Approach

  • Developed an LSTM model for stock-price forecasting.
  • Implemented Black–Litterman portfolio optimization in R.
  • Compared risk and performance against 30 stocks and 100 ETFs using risk-adjusted metrics.

Results & Key Findings

In the project evaluation, the optimized portfolio outperformed 70% of the 100 benchmark ETFs and ranked among the top 20 by Sharpe ratio.

Impact & Conclusion

The project demonstrates a workflow from forecasting to allocation and comparative evaluation. Its value lies in assessing return alongside risk; the reported benchmark results describe the study and do not establish future performance.
